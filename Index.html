WHO I AM

About Me

I'm Anas Al-Homsi, a UX/UI & graphic designer with 5+ years designing user-centered digital products and cohesive visual brands across the UAE and the USA.

My work spans wireframing and prototyping in Figma & Adobe XD, usability testing, and full brand identity systems — with particular depth in bilingual Arabic/English and RTL interface design that most designers never have to solve.

I hold two bachelor's degrees — Business Administration and IT & Software Engineering — meaning I design interfaces for the very software products I also build and ship end to end.
